How to open MacsPane (test build)

1. Drag MacsPane into the Applications folder next to it.

2. Open MacsPane from Applications. macOS will say it can't check it for
   malicious software, because this test build isn't signed by Apple.
   Click Done (not Move to Trash).

3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message
   about MacsPane, and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or
   Touch ID. You only do this once.

4. MacsPane has no Dock icon: it lives in the menu bar, and setup opens the
   first time. In setup, click Grant Access and switch MacsPane on in
   System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. It needs this to
   move and resize other apps' windows, and nothing else. Later test builds
   keep this permission.

To update: quit MacsPane from its menu, drag the new one into Applications
and choose Replace, then open it the same way.

To remove: quit MacsPane from its menu, delete it from Applications, and
delete ~/Library/Application Support/MacsPane.
